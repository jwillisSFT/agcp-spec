# TC-111 — Constitutional Validation

**Requirement**
CR-111 — Governance artifacts SHALL undergo constitutional validation prior to activation.

**Conformance Level**
L3 — Deterministic Governance

### Preconditions

* Compiled governance artifact exists.
* Constitutional validation rules exist.
* Artifact has not yet been activated.

### Request / Scenario

Attempt to activate compiled governance without constitutional validation.

### Simulated Execution Trace

Step 1 — Compiled governance artifact submitted.

Step 2 — Activation requested.

Step 3 — Constitutional validation status checked.

Step 4 — Validation missing.

Step 5 — Activation denied.

### Ledger Delta

```text
CompiledGovernanceSubmitted

ActivationAttempt

ConstitutionalValidationMissing

ActivationDenied
```

### Derived State After Request

```text
NOT_ACTIVE
```

### Expected Result

```http
403 Forbidden
```

### What This Test Proves

Compiled governance cannot govern execution until it has been constitutionally validated.

# TC-115 — Recursive Authority Containment

**Requirement**
CR-115 — Execution authority SHALL remain bounded across recursive delegation chains.

**Conformance Level**
L4 — Execution Authorization Control

### Preconditions

* Agent or actor may delegate authority.
* Delegation chain contains multiple levels.
* Recursive authority bounds are defined.

### Request / Scenario

Actor A delegates to Actor B, who delegates to Actor C with expanded authority.

### Simulated Execution Trace

Step 1 — Initial authority granted to Actor A.

Step 2 — Actor A delegates bounded authority to Actor B.

Step 3 — Actor B attempts recursive delegation to Actor C.

Step 4 — Delegation bounds evaluated.

Step 5 — Expansion beyond original authority detected.

Step 6 — Recursive delegation refused.

### Ledger Delta

```text
AuthorityGranted(A)

Delegation(A,B)

RecursiveDelegationAttempt(B,C)

AuthorityExpansionDetected

Refusal
Reason=RecursiveAuthorityExpansion
```

### Derived State After Request

```text
REFUSED
```

### Expected Result

```http
403 Forbidden
```

### What This Test Proves

Authority may propagate only within explicit bounds and cannot expand recursively without authorization.

# TC-119 — Enforcement Context Availability

**Requirement**
CR-119 — The governance system SHALL make available to the Policy Enforcement Point (PEP) all governance information required to correctly determine execution admissibility.

**Conformance Level**
L4 — Execution Authorization Control

### Preconditions

* A governance decision function exists.
* A Policy Enforcement Point (PEP), integrated or external, is identified or assumed.
* Governance information required for enforcement is defined or inferable from the governance model.

### Request / Scenario

Evaluate whether the governance system makes sufficient governance information available to the PEP for enforcement of execution admissibility.

### Simulated Execution Trace

Step 1 — Governance proposal submitted.

Step 2 — Governance decision function evaluates admissibility.

Step 3 — Required enforcement context is identified.

Step 4 — Governance information made available to the PEP is inspected.

Step 5 — Available information is compared against the governance information required for enforcement.

Step 6 — Sufficiency determination is recorded.

### Ledger Delta

```text
ProposalCreated

AdmissibilityEvaluated

EnforcementContextResolved

PEPContextAvailabilityChecked
````

### Derived State After Request

```text
ENFORCEMENT_CONTEXT_AVAILABLE
```

or

```text
ENFORCEMENT_CONTEXT_INSUFFICIENT
```

### Expected Result

The PEP receives or can access sufficient governance information to determine whether execution is admissible.

### What This Test Proves

Governance enforcement requires that the PEP have access to the governance context needed to enforce admissibility correctly.
