This file contains the current RTM-synchronized AGCP Conformance Test Cases for the indicated range. Each Test Case distinguishes direct test-generating Normative Statements, conditional Normative Statements, and supporting/contextual relationships.

# TC-101 — Continuation Recovery Support

## Purpose

Verify that the implementation satisfies **CR-101** by exercising the controlled scenario below and independently verifying every direct test-generating Normative Statement assigned by the current AGCP Requirements Traceability Matrix.

## Authoritative Traceability

- Primary Conformance Requirement: **CR-101 — Continuation Recovery Support**
- Requirement Text: Governance systems SHALL support recovery of continuation legitimacy through re-derivation, recomputation, reconstruction, or escalation.
- Requirement Group: RG-10 — Continuation Integrity Governance
- Applicable Conformance Level: L4 — HITL & Execution Gating
- Direct Test-Generating NS IDs: NS-13.4-06, NS-13.6C-04
- Conditional NS IDs: None
- Supporting/Contextual NS IDs: None

## Test Objective

A controlled proposal, governance artifact, or implementation condition is configured to exercise CR-101: Governance systems SHALL support recovery of continuation legitimacy through re-derivation, recomputation, reconstruction, or escalation.

## Preconditions

- The implementation is configured for the claimed conformance level and implementation profile.
- All inputs unrelated to the condition under test are valid, current, attributable, and within the same defined evaluation horizon unless a subtest expressly changes them.
- The implementation exposes governance decisions, lifecycle outcomes, enforcement results, and Governance Evidence sufficient for independent assessment.
- Equivalent-input repetitions use the same qualified semantics, governance configuration, applicable policy, Canonical State, and ordering conditions.
- No implementation-specific extension may weaken, replace, or bypass the requirement or any directly mapped Normative Statement.

## Test Inputs and Setup

- Test Scenario: A controlled proposal, governance artifact, or implementation condition is configured to exercise CR-101: Governance systems SHALL support recovery of continuation legitimacy through re-derivation, recomputation, reconstruction, or escalation.
- A committed governed Action loses continuation legitimacy and requests recovery through re-derivation, recomputation, reconstruction, or escalation.
- Authoritative governance configuration, governance policy, Canonical State, tenant and governance domain context, Authority Lineage, and Governance Evidence collection enabled for the scenario.
- Observable interfaces or audit exports sufficient to determine Proposal Qualification, Governance Decision Function, Execution Authorization, Commit Boundary, Continuation Integrity, Autonomous Coordination, Governance Self-Protection, and Governance Evidence behavior where applicable.
- A negative variation that withholds, invalidates, changes, or corrupts the governance input necessary to exercise the mapped Normative Statements.
- Observation Window: Long enough to capture synchronous and asynchronous authoritative outcomes for the processing attempt.
- Negative-Control Rule: Each adversarial or invalid variation changes only the condition identified by the applicable subtest.
