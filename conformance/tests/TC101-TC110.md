# TC-101 — Continuation Recovery Support

**Requirement**
CR-101 — Governance systems SHALL support recovery of continuation legitimacy through re-derivation, recomputation, reconstruction, or escalation.

**Conformance Level**
L4 — Execution Authorization Control

### Preconditions

* Proposal exists in DEGRADED state.
* Recovery policy exists.
* Recovery path is available.

### Request / Scenario

Attempt recovery of degraded proposal.

### Simulated Execution Trace

Step 1 — Proposal exists in DEGRADED state.

Step 2 — Recovery workflow initiated.

Step 3 — Authority re-derived.

Step 4 — Canonical state recomputed.

Step 5 — Evidence re-qualified.

Step 6 — Continuation legitimacy restored.

Step 7 — Proposal returns to AUTHORIZED or PENDING_HITL according to policy.

### Ledger Delta

```text
RecoveryInitiated

AuthorityRederived

StateRecomputed

EvidenceRequalified

ContinuationRecovered

LifecycleStateChanged
From=DEGRADED
To=AUTHORIZED
```

### Derived State After Request

```text
AUTHORIZED
```

or policy-defined recoverable state.

### Expected Result

```http
200 OK
```

### What This Test Proves

The system can restore continuation legitimacy rather than treating all degradation as terminal.
