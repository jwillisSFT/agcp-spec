### TC-001-A01 — NS-7.5-04

**Core Specification Section:** 7.5 Governance Outcomes

**Normative Statement:** A Qualified Proposal that satisfies all applicable governance constraints and invariants SHALL be eligible for an Authorized outcome.

**Relationship:** Specifies — Direct
