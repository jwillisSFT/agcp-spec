2. Verify that the re-evaluation record binds those inputs to Proposal A and the applicable evaluation horizon.
3. Repeat the scenario with one mandatory input unavailable or unqualified.

Expected Result

The conforming path SHALL use the complete applicable continuation input set. The missing-input variation SHALL NOT produce a valid continued-admissibility determination or permit Proposal A to proceed toward commitment.

Pass Criteria

- Each required input is identifiable, attributable, qualified where required, and bound to Proposal A.
- Input versions or references are sufficient to support deterministic reconstruction.
- The missing-input variation produces a governed non-progression outcome and corresponding evidence.

Failure Conditions

- Re-evaluation proceeds using unidentified, stale, unqualified, cross-tenant, or proposal-mismatched inputs.
- A required input is absent without preventing a valid admissibility determination.

### NS-13.3-02

Purpose

Verify that continuation evaluation does not begin until all mandatory inputs applicable to the nonterminal Proposal have been established.

Procedure

1. Withhold the active risk-based governance configuration, the material-change qualification, or another mandatory Proposal A continuation input.
2. Attempt to initiate Risk-Based Re-Evaluation.
3. Restore the missing input and repeat the initiation.

Expected Result

Continuation evaluation SHALL NOT begin while a mandatory input is missing. Once all mandatory inputs are established, evaluation MAY begin in accordance with the active configuration.

Pass Criteria

- No authoritative re-evaluation decision is produced before mandatory inputs are established.
- Proposal A does not proceed to commitment based on an incomplete re-evaluation.
- The non-start outcome and reason are recorded as Governance Evidence.

Failure Conditions

- The implementation begins or completes re-evaluation with incomplete mandatory inputs.
- The implementation silently substitutes default, cached, or inferred input values for required qualified inputs.

### NS-13.4-01

Purpose

Verify detection of the material governance-condition change and deterministic identification of the affected nonterminal Proposal set.

Procedure

1. Submit the E-1 change event under the active risk-based governance configuration.
2. Determine the candidate nonterminal Proposal population within the applicable tenant and governance domain scope.
3. Observe the dependency, scope, lifecycle, and configuration evaluation used to select Proposal A and exclude Proposal B and Proposal C.
4. Replay the same scenario with equivalent qualified inputs on the same or another conformant node.

Expected Result

The implementation SHALL detect the E-1 change, identify Proposal A as affected, and exclude Proposal B and Proposal C from the affected set. Equivalent qualified inputs SHALL produce the same affected set.

Pass Criteria

- Affected-set membership is proposal-specific and explainable from qualified dependency, scope, lifecycle, and configuration information.
- Proposal A is selected; Proposal B and Proposal C are excluded.
- Replay produces the same affected set independent of node, transport, or event-arrival timing that is not itself a qualified governance input.

Failure Conditions

- Proposal A is omitted from the affected set.
- Proposal B or Proposal C is included without a qualified configuration and dependency basis.
- Affected-set membership differs for equivalent qualified inputs.

### NS-13.4-03

Purpose

Verify deterministic re-evaluation of Proposal A after the material E-1 change.

Procedure

1. Initiate re-evaluation for the affected set established under NS-13.4-01.
2. Evaluate Proposal A using current qualified governance inputs and the active configuration.
3. Record the resulting continued-admissibility and lifecycle determination.
4. Replay the same evaluation using equivalent qualified inputs.

Expected Result

Proposal A SHALL be re-evaluated. If the E-1 change removes a condition required for continued admissibility, Proposal A SHALL become non-executable under the applicable lifecycle policy. If all required conditions remain satisfied in a conforming variation, Proposal A MAY remain eligible, but only on the basis of the renewed evaluation. Equivalent qualified inputs SHALL produce equivalent outcomes.

Pass Criteria

- Re-evaluation occurs before Proposal A may proceed to commitment.
- The outcome reflects current governance reality rather than the prior authorization alone.
- Replay reproduces the governance outcome and governing basis.

Failure Conditions

- Proposal A remains eligible solely because it was previously authorized.
- Proposal A proceeds toward commitment before required re-evaluation completes.
- Equivalent qualified inputs produce different re-evaluation outcomes.

### NS-13.4-04

Purpose

Verify that re-evaluation re-derives the governance-significant information required to determine continued admissibility.

Procedure

1. During Proposal A re-evaluation, resolve current authority, applicable governance conditions, required Governance Evidence, and Canonical State where applicable.
2. Compare the re-derived information with the information supporting the prior evaluation or authorization.
3. Verify that changed, revoked, expired, stale, or otherwise unsuitable information does not continue to support admissibility.

Expected Result

The implementation SHALL re-derive the governance-significant information required for Proposal A's continued-admissibility determination. Prior artifacts MAY contribute evidence but SHALL NOT substitute for current qualified authority, conditions, evidence, or Canonical State where required.

Pass Criteria

- Re-derived authority, governance-condition, evidence, and state references are recorded.
- Revocation, expiration, state change, evidence degradation, or scope mismatch is reflected in the outcome.
- The re-evaluation record supports independent reconstruction.

Failure Conditions

- Cached or historical authorization is accepted without renewed derivation where current conditions are required.
- Changed or unsuitable information is ignored.
- The re-derived governance basis is not attributable or replayable.

### NS-13.4-05

Purpose

Verify that nonterminal Proposals outside the affected set are not unnecessarily re-evaluated.

Procedure

1. Observe Proposal B and Proposal C during the E-1 change scenario.
2. Verify that the active configuration and qualified dependency information exclude both Proposals from the affected set.
3. Query re-evaluation records, lifecycle history, authorization state, and Governance Evidence for Proposal B and Proposal C.
4. Repeat with equivalent qualified inputs.

Expected Result

Proposal B and Proposal C SHALL NOT be unnecessarily re-evaluated because the E-1 change does not affect their continued execution admissibility under the active risk-based governance configuration. Their lifecycle and authorization state SHALL remain unchanged except for independently governed events unrelated to this test.

Pass Criteria

- No re-evaluation decision or lifecycle mutation is recorded for Proposal B or Proposal C as a consequence of the E-1 change.
- Governance Evidence records or permits reconstruction of their exclusion from the affected set.
- Equivalent qualified inputs reproduce the same exclusion result.

Failure Conditions

- Proposal B or Proposal C is re-evaluated merely because a governance event occurred.
- An unaffected Proposal's lifecycle, authorization, or commit eligibility is changed without an independent governance basis.
- The implementation cannot establish why an unaffected Proposal was excluded or included.

### NS-13.7-02

Purpose

Verify the complete observable conformance behavior required for Risk-Based Re-Evaluation.

Procedure

1. Execute Tests A and B in full.
2. Verify detection of the material change, application of active risk-based governance configuration, affected-set determination, deterministic re-evaluation, avoidance of unnecessary re-evaluation, lifecycle handling of inadmissible affected Proposals, protection of commit eligibility, and evidence generation.
3. Verify that an affected Proposal lacking a verified continuation basis cannot commit.

Expected Result

The implementation SHALL satisfy all applicable continuation-conformance behaviors for the tested scenario and SHALL record the re-evaluation determination and resulting lifecycle effects as Governance Evidence.

Pass Criteria

- The E-1 change is detected and qualified.
- Proposal A is deterministically identified and re-evaluated.
- Proposal B and Proposal C are not unnecessarily re-evaluated.
- An inadmissible Proposal A is transitioned to a policy-defined non-executable lifecycle state and cannot commit.
- Re-evaluation determinations and lifecycle effects are attributable, integrity-protected, and replayable.

Failure Conditions

- Any required behavior is omitted, bypassed, performed non-deterministically, or unsupported by sufficient evidence.
- An affected Proposal lacking a verified continuation basis remains commit-eligible.

### NS-13.7-03

Purpose

Verify that failures of affected-proposal selection, required re-evaluation, unaffected-proposal protection, continuation validity, or deterministic processing are treated as non-conformance.

Procedure

1. Exercise the following controlled failure variations separately:
   - omit Proposal A from the affected set;
   - allow Proposal A to proceed without re-evaluation;
   - unnecessarily re-evaluate Proposal B or Proposal C contrary to the active configuration;
   - allow Proposal A to continue under invalid governance conditions; or
   - produce different affected sets or outcomes for equivalent qualified inputs.
2. Record whether the conformance assessment detects each prohibited behavior.

Expected Result

Each controlled failure variation SHALL fail TC-122 and SHALL constitute non-conformance with the applicable AGCP requirements.

Pass Criteria

- Every prohibited variation is detected.
- None of the prohibited variations is reported as a passing implementation behavior.
- The failure evidence identifies the affected Proposal, configuration, change event, observed behavior, and violated normative statement.

Failure Conditions

- Any prohibited variation passes TC-122.
- The implementation or assessment process suppresses, normalizes, or treats the prohibited behavior as implementation discretion.

- Authorization State: Prior authorization does not constitute unconditional future execution authority for Proposal A. Proposal B and Proposal C are not invalidated merely because an unrelated material change occurred.
- Commit Eligibility: Proposal A cannot commit until required re-evaluation establishes a valid continuation basis and final Commit-Bound Admissibility is resolved. Unaffected Proposals remain governed by their existing continuation and commit-time requirements.

## Notes / Rationale
TC-122 is a single conformance test containing two inseparable control paths. Test A verifies required re-evaluation of affected nonterminal Proposals. Test B verifies protection of unaffected nonterminal Proposals from unnecessary re-evaluation. Both paths are necessary to establish that Risk-Based Re-Evaluation is selective, deterministic, governed by active risk-based configuration, and capable of preserving continued execution admissibility without converting every governance-condition change into indiscriminate global re-evaluation.
