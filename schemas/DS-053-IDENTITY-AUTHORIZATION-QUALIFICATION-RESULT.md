# DS-053 - Identity and Authorization Qualification Result

DS-053 is the canonical result of resolving an authenticated subject through the implementation-selected IAS Profile. It records authoritative account status, roles, groups, entitlements, authorization attributes, governance permissions, scope, validity/freshness/revocation, authoritative source basis, and qualification evidence. Identity-system authorization in DS-053 is not AGCP Execution Authorization and does not itself constitute a Governance Decision.
